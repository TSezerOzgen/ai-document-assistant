FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Do you see children?
A: Yes. Dr. Raman treats patients from age 3 and up.

Q: I'm nervous about the dentist. Do you offer sedation?
A: Yes, we offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) at $95 per visit. Oral
   sedation is available for longer procedures; discuss it at your exam.

Q: Does the numbing injection hurt?
A: We apply a topical numbing gel first, so most patients feel only
   slight pressure.

Q: How long does an implant take start to finish?
A: The post is placed in one visit. Healing takes 3-4 months. The crown
   is then fitted over two more visits.

Q: How many visits is a root canal?
A: Usually two. Complex molars occasionally need three.

Q: Will whitening damage my enamel?
A: No. In-office whitening does not harm enamel. Some patients have mild
   sensitivity for a day or two.

Q: Do you speak languages other than English?
A: Yes, we have Spanish-speaking staff available Monday through Friday.

Q: Can I pay in installments?
A: Yes, through CareCredit — 0% APR for 12 months on treatment over
   $1,000, subject to credit approval.

Q: What if I don't have insurance?
A: Uninsured patients receive a 10% discount when paying in full at the
   time of service.
